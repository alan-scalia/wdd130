Rogue River Rafting

Cascading along the vibrant, forested slopes of Oregon's Pacific coast range, a Rogue River rafting adventure winds its way down from snow-capped volcanic peaks, mirroring the playful spirit of the river otters that call this river home. Deer, black bears, and other wildlife thrive along this federally protected Wild & Scenic River, creating a pristine environment for explorers. With inviting water temperatures, rafters can dive in for a swim or navigate the exciting rapids on a 3 or 4-day trip. From May through August, each evening is spent in cozy riverside lodges, while September and October bring nights of riverside camping under the stars.

Snake River Rafting

The Snake River carves out the deepest gorge in the United States, Hell's Canyon, reaching impressive depths of up to 7,900 feet in certain areas. Over time, boulders and rock slides have tumbled into the river, creating some of the most thrilling whitewater rapids in the Pacific Northwest.

A multi-day whitewater rafting trip is the ideal way to experience the Snake River as it flows through Hells Canyon. This journey offers the perfect blend of Idaho's whitewater adventure, stunning geological formations, and fascinating natural history.

Salmon River Rafting

The Salmon River spans 425 miles, draining over 14,000 square miles and descending more than 7,000 vertical feet from its high-altitude headwaters. Wild and untamed, the Salmon River was one of the first rivers to be designated Wild and Scenic by Congress, carving its path through rugged Idaho landscapes and offering unique rafting trips like no other. Flowing through the vast Frank Church - River of No Return Wilderness, the Salmon River winds through the second-deepest gorge in North America. Divided into sections known as the Main Salmon, the Middle Fork, and the Lower Salmon, each offers an ideal combination of thrilling rapids, gentle floats, excellent swimming, and serene camping, perfect for a memorable family or group adventure.
